# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ukrainian beauty professionals (hair stylists, nail artists, brow masters, makeup artists) running solo or small-team studios. Primary context: mobile phone, mid-session or between clients, needing fast task completion. Secondary context: desktop after hours for analytics and CRM. Average age 22-35. Tech-comfortable but not tech-obsessed. Time is their revenue — every extra tap costs money.

## Product Purpose

Bookit is a booking management SaaS for Ukrainian beauty industry. The tagline: "Твій розумний link in bio, який заробляє гроші." It replaces paper notebooks and Instagram DMs with a professional booking system that also functions as a public link-in-bio page for clients. Success = master gets more bookings, fewer no-shows, and earns more per hour without spending time on admin.

Tiers: Starter (free), Pro (700₴/mo), Studio (299₴/master/mo for teams).

## Brand Personality

Warm authority. Quietly premium. Like a luxury beauty studio, not a tech startup. Three words: intimate, precise, elevated.

Emotional goals: masters feel proud showing their Bookit page to clients (not embarrassed by a generic tool). Clients feel reassured they're booking with a professional.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS blue (Calendly, Acuity, SimplyBook) — cold, clinical, interchangeable
- Neon fintech dark mode — wrong audience energy
- Generic white-card-on-white-bg dashboards with blue primary buttons
- Instagram/TikTok maximalism — too noisy for a tool used mid-work
- Purple "AI startup" glow aesthetic
- Any design that could belong to a project management tool (Notion, Linear aesthetic applied here = wrong)

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over completeness** — the master needs the answer in one glance, one tap. If reading is required, the hierarchy failed.
2. **Warmth without softness** — the palette is warm (cream, brown, sand) but the interface is precise and trustworthy. Cozy ≠ unprofessional.
3. **Script as signature, sans as worker** — Great Vibes appears only at emotional/identity moments (logo, greeting). The rest is DM Sans or Geist. Mixing them everywhere dilutes both.
4. **Color earns weight** — the bold color block (hero card, accent) means "act now." Neutral glass means "info, no urgency." Never invert this.
5. **Mobile-first always** — designed for iPhone, tested on iPhone, scaled to desktop. Never the reverse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Ukrainian language throughout (Cyrillic). Touch targets ≥ 44×44px for all interactive elements. No reliance on color alone to convey state (dot + badge text for status). Support for both light (Blossom) and dark (Studio) themes — user-selectable.
